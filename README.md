# Sunday 22 November 2026 — the whole day in one picture

One square poster carrying a full day of world news: 26 stories from 9
regions, every one of them from Sunday 22 November 2026 (UTC), gathered
from the wires as they stood at 15:00 UTC. The three biggest stories get
full illustrated panels up top; ten more follow as cards, thirteen as a
brief list, and a small map pins the thirteen illustrated stories to the
places they happened. A "not today" strip keeps five big stories from
other days quarantined under their real dates, and every story is
labelled CONFIRMED, OFFICIAL or CLAIM so the reader can see how sure
each one is.

The day's top stories:

- **COP31 pact collapses** — the final draft drops the words "fossil
  fuels" for a 35%-by-2035 power goal; the EU objects and the deal is
  not adopted.
- **Verstappen by 0.639s** — 35 lead changes in 50 laps at Las Vegas,
  Norris second and Piastri third; a third Vegas win in four years.
- **Baltic blaze burns on** — the Sparta II, struck on Saturday, is
  still burning off Loviisa with her 16 crew safe, and the water being
  pumped into her may sink her.
- **Drones pound Khartoum; UN hit** — UN premises are struck in the
  Sudanese capital and one person is killed.
- **3 soup-kitchen workers killed** — a Gaza soup kitchen is hit and
  Hamas seeks urgent talks.
