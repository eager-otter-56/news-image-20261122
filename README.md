# Sunday 22 November 2026 — the day in one image

One poster, 25 stories, 9 regions: everything that happened on Sunday 22 November 2026 (UTC), drawn as a single newspaper page with big illustrated scenes, a world map, and a one-line date check for the big stories that belong to another day.

- The climate summit in Antalya collapsed after its final draft left out the words "fossil fuels" entirely, ending the longest COP on record with no deal.
- Max Verstappen won a 35-lead-change Las Vegas Grand Prix by just 0.639 seconds over Lando Norris.
- The drone-struck cargo ship Sparta II was towed out of Finnish waters, her sixteen crew safe, after a multinational rescue in the Gulf of Finland.
- Carlos Alcaraz beat world number one Alexander Zverev in straight sets to win his first ATP Finals title in Turin.
- Florida's St. Johns River topped its Hurricane Ian record above Lake Harney, with the crest still to come overnight.
