# Sunday 22 November 2026 — the day in one image

One picture that sums up a full day of world news: 26 stories from 9 regions, every one of them from Sunday 22 November 2026 (UTC), drawn from the wires as reported by 15:00 UTC. Big type throughout, a consistent colour per region, and no frames between stories — each panel sits on the page separated by air and its region tint. A world map pins the thirteen lead stories where they happened.

The day's top stories:

- **COP31 pact collapses** — the final draft drops the words "fossil fuels" for a 35%-by-2035 power goal; the EU objects and the deal is not adopted.
- **Verstappen wins Las Vegas by 0.639s** — 35 lead changes in 50 laps, Norris second and Piastri third; a third Vegas win in four years.
- **Baltic blaze burns on** — the Sparta II, struck Saturday, still burns off Loviisa on day two; all 16 crew are safe, but pumped-in water may sink her.
- **Drones pound Khartoum; the UN is hit** — homes wrecked and UN premises struck, one killed, as the fighting grinds on.
- **Iran claims a US drone downed** — the Guard says an MQ-1 fell east of Hormuz; carried as a CLAIM, one side's account only.

Every story is labelled CONFIRMED (two or more sources), OFFICIAL (an official statement), CLAIM (one side's account only) or DEVELOPING (still unfolding at 15:00 UTC). Big stories that belong to another day are quarantined in a NOT TODAY — DATE CHECK strip under their real dates, so nothing from 19–21 November leaks into Sunday's page.

Entry for the make.fun competition [news-image-20261122](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261122).
