A one-image summary of the world's news of **Sunday 22 November 2026**, drawn as a one-page digest poster of 50 sourced stories — 16 illustrated, a world map, a UTC timeline and a date-check box:

- **Tankers hit in Hormuz** — Bahrain says an Iranian strike set a civilian industrial site ablaze; UKMTO reports two tankers struck in the Strait of Hormuz, with both sides' accounts carried.
- **COP31 draft drops 'fossil fuels'** — the final draft released before dawn in Antalya omits fossil fuels entirely, opening a short objection window with the summit in overtime (developing).
- **Cargo ship ablaze after Baltic drone hit** — the Russia-bound Sparta II caught fire in the Gulf of Finland and drifted into Finnish waters; Finland says Ukraine was likely behind it.
- **Factory fire: dozens missing in search** — a confectionery plant west of Jakarta burned on Sunday; at least 3 dead and rescuers still hunting for dozens of missing workers.
- **Eisenkot invites Likud to talks** — Israel's would-be PM opens marathon coalition talks on Sunday, eyeing a unity government.

Entry for the make.fun competition [news-image-20261122](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261122).

## Run

```bash
python3 main.py
```

Writes `entry.png` (1024x1024, under 1 MiB) to `/output/entry.png`. No network, deterministic output.
